Artifact Note: [[020 Learning is an apprenticeship to signs]]

# Journal

2026-07-13 Wk 29 Mon - 00:58 +03:00

Idea inspired from semiotics writing that often clarifies an inversion of agency in the practice of meaning making. We all participate and co-create shared system of meaning. So it is important to see signs as more than simply personal artifacts.

Similarly, learning with peers and teachers is co-navigating the environment with them. The obstacles we encounter are not unique to us personally and this is what allows us to study the pedagogy aspects of the practice.

Inversion of agency: I learn $\to$ Learning is part of the meaning structure that shapes me.

Agency is important; we have to actively participate in these systems of meaning, but there are always structures we're participating in, so agency has more to do with our ability to see them and make informed decisions based on them rather than just a claim of owning or being the source of them.
