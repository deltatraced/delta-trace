Just as in the title of this project, `000 Atomic`, an atomic context or note is to be interpreted to mean that either

1. It is singular in purpose and does not mix multiple concerns. In a sense, it is basic (like a single task, a single question, a single reported issue, a single proposition, a single concept, etc.)
2. OR It is a singular composition of other basic notes. Some notes synthesize information of others, and so form a singular concern of synthesis of information. Think like overview notes, judgment notes which take reasons and evidence into consideration, goal notes which decide many tasks that will be prioritized for a software release, and so on.

Atomicity of context is balanced against locality of context: [[017 Notes should have high locality of context]]

Make use of [[002 Serial Reduction of Context]] to identify stages with high coupling of information to group them together, and [[003 Parallel Reduction of Context]] to identify independent stages that could be worked on independently and have lower coupling to separate them and increase atomicity.
