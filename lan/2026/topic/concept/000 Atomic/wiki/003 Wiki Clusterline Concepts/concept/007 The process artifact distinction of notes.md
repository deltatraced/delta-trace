This note taking system has broadly two types of note files: 
- [[000 Term Process Note]]
- [[008 Term Artifact Note]]

In general, process notes are written with the expectation of immutability, and to act as historical evidence of some artifact being created.

A software project consists of a set of artifacts; source code, build scripts, and so on. Process notes are often about some the creation or modification of some artifact and are meant to record the knowledge work involved in maintaining them.

A key distinction of a process note is that its content is in a `# Journal` header. Usually other headers are artifact notes, such as a summary synthesis, an overview, a final solution, and so on.
