In order to draw apt judgments and succeed in our practice, we ought to respect [[006 Signs of our practice|signs of our practice]].

In any given context, respecting its signs means providing it with the necessary attention, commitment, and deliberation.

Respect is an activity of attention and not of productivity. In contrast to optimization, which is an activity of productivity, respecting the signs of our practice provides us with higher quality data and interpretation thereof, which may then power our search for optimal ends.
