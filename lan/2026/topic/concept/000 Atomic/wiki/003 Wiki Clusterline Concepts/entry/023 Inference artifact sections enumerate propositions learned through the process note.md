We have an `Inference` note type which records learnings and facts distilled from investigations and other evidence generated by [[000 Term Process Note|process notes]]. 

In a given process note, the `# Inference` section lists key facts discovered in the process note, which later can be referenced in decision making.

This is in contrast to [[022 Decision artifact sections enumerate key facts about changes and commitments produced by the process note]]:

`# Decision` references facts about a change produced by the process note and `# Inference` references facts encountered independent of any changes the process note made.

Notes of type `Investigation` can also produce an `# Inference` section that settles their title denoted objective as they are progressed to completion. As there can be multiple inferences that come together as an argument, this should be interpreted that the investigation is settled by the last inference listed under `# Inference`.
