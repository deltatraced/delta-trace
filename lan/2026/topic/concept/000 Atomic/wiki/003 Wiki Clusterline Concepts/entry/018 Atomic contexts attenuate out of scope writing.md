By paying [[005 Respecting the signs of a context|respect]] and attending to a declared context, we attenuate unrelated writing or scope leakage.

This allows notes to act as a unit of attention, and attenuate noise from the outside world of considerations and distractions.

Of course, notes are interconnected and can engage in context sharing. Notes may spawn other contexts, and we may need to address other considerations that are prompted by the current consideration. But those also are attenuated in the presence of this note, as they are sent somewhere else, outside this page, and the transitions are made explicit before us.
