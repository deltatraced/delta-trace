
# 1 Definition

## 1.1 Wiki

See the wiki: [Union (set theory)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Union_(set_theory)).

A [[001 Set|set]] $A \cup B$ is said to be a union of $A$ and $B$ and is defined in [[009 Set builder notation|set builder notation]] as

$$

A \cup B \equiv \lbrace a \in U \suchthat a \in A \lor a \in B \rbrace

$$

which can be read as "$A$ union $B$ is equivalent to the set of all $a$ in the [[006 compliment#^term-universe|universe]] such that $a$ is in $A$ or $a$ is in $B$."
