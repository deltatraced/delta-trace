We start with defining the [[000 Defn N|natural numbers]]. Already with the first two axioms, we can prove [[000 three_nat 3 is a natural number|3 is a natural number]]. But these two axioms are not enough to pin down the natural numbers, since they do not prevent wrap-around. We prove that we [[001 four_nz 4 is not 0|4 is not 0]] to show that at least we cannot wrap back to 0.
